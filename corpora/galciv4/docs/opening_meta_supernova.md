Core Competitive Mechanics:

1. Turn 1 Capital City Instant Build:
   - Capital City has +0 Manufacturing Cost and does NOT wait in build queue.
   - It must be placed on Turn 1 on your homeworld to immediately gain +10 Colony Gross Income, +6 Pop Cap, +3 Sensor Range, +1 Control/turn, +100 Influence, and +1 Culture Point/turn.

2. Minister Allocation Meta:
   - Exploration: Assign leader with highest Diligence. Directly increases ship movement speed across all fleets. Speed is everything in early expansion.
   - Technology: Assign leader with highest Tech/Intelligence. Increases monthly research point generation.
   - Colonization: Assign leader with highest Social. Accelerates population growth on core worlds.

3. Executive Order Synergy:
   - 'Draft Colonists': -33 Control, spawns free Colony Ship with passenger on Turn 1.
   - 'Telescope Takeover': -10 Control, immediately clears fog of war around prospective high-class habitable worlds.
   - 'Print Money': Generates emergency cash reserves to rush improvements or recruit key leaders.

4. Manual Probe Scouting:
   - Never set probes to auto-explore in early turns. The auto-explore AI walks into hostile raiders and pirates.
   - Manually vector probes toward G-type (Yellow) stars to locate Class 20+ terran/oceanic planets, and Purple stars to locate Promethion.

5. Feeder Colony Pre-Seeding:
   - When preparing a new Core World, settle the minor feeder worlds in the system first.
   - Do not appoint a Governor on the main world until feeder colonies are sending raw inputs, giving the new Core World immediate 20+ production on Turn 1 of governor appointment.

6. Starbase 3-Node Clustering:
   - Take 'Mobility Rights' early (+2 starbase range).
   - Carefully place mining starbases equidistant from 3 resource nodes (e.g. Durantium, Promethion, Ascension Crystals) to capture all 3 with a single starbase.

7. Continuous Shipyard Pipeline:
   - Orbital shipyards have zero pollution and zero maintenance penalty. Keep queues full 100% of the time: Probes -> Colony Ships -> Constructors -> Survey Ships -> Frigates.
