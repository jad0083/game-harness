# Advanced Gameplay Guide for High-Level Difficulty AI Settings
Source: https://www.galciv4.com/article/512126/advanced-game-play-guide-for-high-level-difficulty-ai-settings
License: not stated by the source page; kept for personal reference, verify before redistributing.

Summary:
Proven competitive strategy for beating Galactic Civilizations IV on Incredible difficulty with distant AI proximity, normal pacing, and abundant galactic sectors.

Key Principles:
1. Sol Starting System:
   - Sol is the premier starting system in the game due to numerous dead worlds (Mars, Venus, Mercury, Saturn, Jupiter) available to convert/colonize as feeder worlds and an abundant asteroid belt.
2. Ministers & Character Assignments:
   - Assign highest tech leader as Minister of Technology immediately on Turn 1.
   - Assign leader with high diligence as Minister of Exploration (grants direct ship movement bonus across all fleets).
   - Assign high social leader as Minister of Colonization for population growth.
3. Turn-by-Turn Opening Sequence:
   - Turn 1:
     * Earth: Queue Industrial Center, followed by Capital Mainframe.
     * Executive Orders: Execute 'Draft Colonists' (spawns free Colony Ship) and 'Telescope Takeover' (reveals habitable worlds).
     * Ideology: Spend point on 'Self Governance' for +10% approval and a free leader.
     * Policies: Select 'Asteroid Exploitation' and 'Heart of the Empire'. Set taxes to low for high citizen approval and production.
     * Research: Start with 'Asteroid Mining' (or 'Space Elevators').
     * Fleets: Join Commander ships with Flagship/Survey ship to harvest artifact anomalies. Send probes toward nearest purple star (for Promethion) and yellow stars.
     * Shipyard: Queue Colony Ship and Probe.
   - Turn 2: Survey anomalies, probe outward.
   - Turn 3:
     * Asteroid Mining finishes -> select 'Starbases'.
     * Dispatch asteroid miners to 2 closest belts. Earth jumps to 20-25 production immediately.
   - Turn 5:
     * Starbase tech completes -> select 'Hyperwave Radio'.
     * Colonize Mars with Colony Ship.
     * Ideology/Culture: Take 'Mobility Rights' (+2 starbase range), allowing a single starbase to mine 3 resource nodes simultaneously.
     * Earth finishes initial build: queue Entertainment District and Manufacturing Districts adjacent to capital.
   - Turn 6:
     * Shipyard: Queue Colony ship, Asteroid miner, Probe, Constructor.
     * Send probes to nearest yellow stars (G-class) to identify Class 20-30 worlds for Core World #2.
   - Turn 7:
     * Hyperwave Radio completes -> select 'Subspace Scanning' (for Eyes of the Universe wonder).
     * Policy slot: Coerced Colonization (+100% growth, -5% approval).
   - Turn 8-11:
     * Survey ship upgrade: Communications Terminal (+2 sensor range). Commander upgrade: Crew Quarters (+HP).
     * Collect Durantium and Promethion for wonders (Elon's Lift requires 5 Durantium; Kazar's Mainframe requires 5 Promethion).
     * Research 'Space Elevators' -> build Elon's Lift.
   - Turn 12-14:
     * Research 'Research Districts' -> build Kazar's Mainframe.
     * Research 'Anomaly Detection' -> unlocks dedicated Survey Ships.
   - Turn 15-20:
     * Identify Class 20-30 Core World candidate. Colonize surrounding feeder planets first so the world starts with massive manufacturing inputs upon governor appointment.
     * Path to Crime Elimination: Research Leadership Recruiting -> Colonial Bureaucracy -> Political Capital -> Colonial Law and Order.
   - Turn 21-35:
     * Planetology -> Xeno Manufacturing -> Starbase Modules.
     * Build Durantium Processor on Earth.
     * Cluster planetary districts by type (Manufacturing next to Capital and mineral tiles; Research clustered around Mainframes).
