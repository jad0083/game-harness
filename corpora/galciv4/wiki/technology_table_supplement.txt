# GalCiv 4: Technology Table Reference & Prerequisites
Source: https://wiki.galciv.com/index.php?title=GalCiv4:Technology_Table

Key Tech Branches & Crucial Unlocks:

Engineering:
- Asteroid Mining: Unlocks Asteroid Miner ship class, unlocks asteroid exploitation (+1-2 production per belt).
- Starbases: Unlocks Mining Starbase, Starbase Defense System, Starbase Modules.
- Space Elevators: Cost -42 Research. Unlocks Space Elevator improvement, Elon's Lift wonder (+10% all planetary manufacturing, requires 5 Durantium).
- Sublight Drives: Cost -42 Research. Unlocks Sublight Drives ship engine component (+1 movement speed).
- Subspace Scanning: Cost -80 Research. Unlocks Eyes of the Universe wonder (reveals entire map, requires 5 Promethion).
- Orbital Manufacturing: Cost -80 Research. Unlocks advanced Shipyard Modules and constructor capacity.

Colonization:
- Research Districts: Cost -42 Research. Unlocks Research District improvement, Kazar's Mainframe wonder (+25% research, requires 5 Promethion).
- Planetology: Cost -80 Research. Unlocks Xeno Manufacturing (+10% max manufacturing).
- Extreme Miniaturization: Cost -250 Research. Unlocks Battleship Module, Battleship Ship Category, Large Ship Hulls.
- Terraforming: Cost -128 Research. Unlocks Atmospheric Scrubbers, Soil Enrichment, Planetary Recycling Center (-430 manufacturing cost, -0.05 pollution).

Warfare:
- Armed Shuttles: Cost -42 Research. Unlocks Deflectors, Point Defense, Hull Plating components, Barrier Reef improvement, Defensive Measures policy.
- Weapon Systems: Cost -42 Research. Unlocks Lasers, Railguns, Enhanced Particle Beams, Fighter Ship Category, Bomber Ship Category.
- Pulse Cannons: Cost -80 Research. Unlocks Enhanced Pulse Cannon, +10% Kinetic Attack.
- Gravimetric Shields: Cost -242 Research. Unlocks +10% Shield Strength.
- Invulnerability Shields: Cost -500 Research. Unlocks Fleet Shield Tech.

Governance:
- Leadership Recruiting: Cost -42 Research. Unlocks Invite Leaders executive order, allows recruitment of 3 additional leaders.
- Colonial Bureaucracy: Cost -60 Research. Unlocks Colonial Bureaucracy policy, increases governor limit.
- Political Capital: Cost -80 Research. Unlocks Draft Colonists executive order (-33 Control), Illicit Deals executive order (+600 Credits).
- Colonial Law & Order: Cost -120 Research. Eliminates planetary crime generation across empire.
- Hyperwave Radio: Cost -42 Research. Unlocks +1 Empire Policy Slot.
