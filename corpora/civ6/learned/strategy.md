# Learned strategy rules

Written by the pilot app during play; promote proven items into the main corpus.

- A neighbour has 3x or more our military strength and our cities are marked threatened -> build ranged defenders (slinger/archer) in every city before settlers or wonders  
  _why:_ strategy review T41 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- Treasury at or below the reserve -> no purchases; put production into defence and settlers instead  
  _why:_ strategy review T41 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- Playing China -> pick techs and civics whose eureka or inspiration can be triggered soon, since China's boosts give 50%  
  _why:_ strategy review T41 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- When a city is threatened by enemy units, immediately switch its production to a defensive combat unit like a Slinger or Archer.  
  _why:_ strategy review T43 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- A threatened city with production under 5 and a defender taking 5 or more turns -> buy the defender with gold if the treasury stays above the reserve  
  _why:_ strategy review T55 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- An idle Trader early on -> send it to a foreign city first: under Kublai Khan this triggers Gerege and the Currency eureka in one move  
  _why:_ strategy review T55 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_
