# Learned strategy rules

Written by the pilot app during play; promote proven items into the main corpus.

- A neighbour has 3x or more our military strength and our cities are marked threatened -> build ranged defenders (slinger/archer) in every city before settlers or wonders  
  _why:_ strategy review T41 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- Treasury at or below the reserve -> no purchases; put production into defence and settlers instead  
  _why:_ strategy review T41 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- Playing China -> pick techs and civics whose eureka or inspiration can be triggered soon, since China's boosts give 50%  
  _why:_ strategy review T41 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- When a city is threatened by enemy units, immediately switch its production to a defensive combat unit like a Slinger or Archer.  
  _why:_ strategy review T43 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- A threatened city with production under 5 and a defender taking 5 or more turns -> buy the defender with gold if the treasury stays above the reserve  
  _why:_ strategy review T55 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- An idle Trader early on -> send it to a foreign city first: under Kublai Khan this triggers Gerege and the Currency eureka in one move  
  _why:_ strategy review T55 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- No civic selected -> pick the one that leads to the next government (State Workforce toward Political Philosophy)  
  _why:_ strategy review T55 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-26)_

- When a city has empty production (e.g. Chengdu building NOTHING), assign a build order immediately to prevent the turn from stalling.  
  _why:_ strategy review T61 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When military strength is high (99) but multiple cities are threatened by enemy units in peacetime, these are likely barbarians; maintain existing defensive units and shift new production to growth.  
  _why:_ strategy review T61 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Civic shows 'none' -> queue a civic that same turn; an empty civic slot wastes all culture and caused the civics_known miss at T72.  
  _why:_ strategy review T73 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- City builds NOTHING while threatened -> order a Monument or a defender that turn instead of leaving it to the AI.  
  _why:_ strategy review T73 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Gold above 200 with income under 3 per turn -> buy a Monument or Builder in the weakest city rather than letting it sit.  
  _why:_ strategy review T73 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When gold yield turns negative due to unit maintenance, slot the Conscription policy to reduce costs.  
  _why:_ strategy review T85 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When the capital is idle and expansion targets are missed, prioritize building a Settler over other districts.  
  _why:_ strategy review T85 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When facing an AI with significantly lower military strength, maintain a strong garrison in threatened cities while advancing towards their territory.  
  _why:_ strategy review T85 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- A city showing NOTHING in production is filled the same turn with a Builder or Trader; idle turns are what cost the cities milestone.  
  _why:_ strategy review T99 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Faith stock above 500 with no religion in reach -> buy worship buildings (Shrine, later Temple) instead of banking further.  
  _why:_ strategy review T99 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When a Settler is 4 turns out and a civic completes near it, re-slot Colonization in an economic slot at that civic's completion.  
  _why:_ strategy review T99 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When facing a sudden gold deficit from unit upkeep, slot Conscription or similar maintenance-reducing policies in a wildcard slot as soon as possible to save the economy.  
  _why:_ strategy review T123 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When a weaker neighbor declares a surprise war, rely on the existing army and walls to defend, seeking peace rather than overbuilding units and bankrupting the empire.  
  _why:_ strategy review T123 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- A city-state declares war as a side effect of a major civ's war, and our military is at least twice the enemy's -> do not rewrite the strategy; defend with the existing army and treat peace with the major civ as the exit condition.  
  _why:_ strategy review T124 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Treasury below the reserve during a war -> defend through city production and free policy swaps (Conscription), not gold purchases.  
  _why:_ strategy review T143 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Faith stockpile above 300 with no religion of our own -> buy faith buildings (temples, worship buildings) in follower cities instead of leaving it idle.  
  _why:_ strategy review T143 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Enemy military strength above ours while at war -> the best production cities switch from wonders to the cheapest strong unit (Crouching Tiger/Crossbowman) until strength is back above the enemy's.  
  _why:_ strategy review T143 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Research or civic slot empty at a review -> fill it the same turn with a boosted choice; an idle queue for even a few turns costs tech milestones.  
  _why:_ strategy review T156 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Faith stock above 500 with no religion goal -> name a concrete faith purchase (worship building, temple) each review; unused faith has zero value.  
  _why:_ strategy review T156 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Military rank 1 with gold income under +5 -> slot maintenance-cutting military cards (Conscription) instead of queuing more units.  
  _why:_ strategy review T156 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When under attack and outmatched in military strength, switch threatened cities from slow infrastructure (like a 12-turn castle in Haarlem) to immediate defensive units like Crossbowmen.  
  _why:_ strategy review T171 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When a city is idle with empty production, promptly assign a unique or high-value building (like Government Plaza buildings) to maintain momentum.  
  _why:_ strategy review T171 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Accumulated faith above 1000 should be spent on available religious buildings (like Temples) to convert stockpile into yields.  
  _why:_ strategy review T171 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When the briefing shows 'Research: none' or a city building NOTHING, issue the research or production order in the same review before anything else.  
  _why:_ strategy review T184 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When a great person race is lost in a class where we have under half the points needed, do not redirect effort to it; keep feeding the class we lead (here, scientists).  
  _why:_ strategy review T184 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- A finished Campus without a Library comes before a Castle in any city that is not threatened.  
  _why:_ strategy review T184 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When falling behind in Great Person races or tech parity, switch from long wonders or district projects to high-yield buildings like Universities.  
  _why:_ strategy review T197 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Always check for idle cities (building NOTHING) and empty research queues after a war or major event.  
  _why:_ strategy review T197 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Do not order Trader production if no additional trade route capacity is available (e.g. from a Commercial Hub or Harbor).  
  _why:_ In Turn 202, a previous order to build a Trader was refused by the AI because the civ only had 1 Trader unit and 0 Commercial Hubs/Harbors, hitting the base capacity of 1. _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Research shows 'none' in the briefing -> issue a research order the same turn, choosing a tech whose Eureka we can trigger (China's boosts are 50%).  
  _why:_ strategy review T210 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- A Great Person race is lost more than once while the Great Person slot holds a military card -> at the next civic completion, swap in the matching points card (Inspiration or Literary Tradition) instead of chasing the race with production.  
  _why:_ strategy review T210 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Treasury above 400 with gold yield above 10 and a University or Amphitheater taking more than 10 turns -> buy it within the treasury share rather than hoarding.  
  _why:_ strategy review T210 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When entering a new Era with a high Era Score threshold (e.g. 76 for a normal age), immediately prioritize new building types and Great People to secure the necessary points.  
  _why:_ strategy review T223 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- If Great Person races like Scientist and Writer are repeatedly lost, utilize wildcard slots for policy:inspiration and policy:literary_tradition to aggressively boost Great Person point generation.  
  _why:_ strategy review T223 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When techs_known or civics_known lags, progress the cheapest leftover older tech or civic first (e.g. Naval Tradition, Military Training, Cartography). This raises the count quickly and each civic completion opens a free policy swap.  
  _why:_ strategy review T237 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When a city has a district without its first building (Campus without Library), that building comes before a Settler or a long wonder in that city.  
  _why:_ strategy review T237 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Faith stock above 300 with no named use should go into Holy Site buildings, which faith can buy, rather than sitting idle.  
  _why:_ strategy review T237 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When science or production milestones are missed because a city queue empties or the AI starts a wonder, issue direct production orders for the missing infrastructure (e.g. Library, University) at the start of the next turn.  
  _why:_ strategy review T251 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- When a free policy swap unlocks and a science milestone is missed, replace adjacency-based cards (like Natural Philosophy) with building-based boosters (like Rationalism) if the cities have enough population or districts to benefit.  
  _why:_ strategy review T251 _(google:gemini-3.8-flash, 2026-09-27)_

- Gold per turn negative and treasury below reserve -> cut maintenance first (Conscription, stop Castles/Walls/Museums), queue Traders and a Commercial Hub, and pick a civic at once so policy swaps are free.
